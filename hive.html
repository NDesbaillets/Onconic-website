The Hive

A collaborative space for oncology teams.

Like a hive, Onconic works because each member contributes. The Hive brings together training material, tumor-specific pages and practical tools for physicians and medical assistants. Access is reserved to verified members.

  • Training: guidelines, internal presentations, literature summaries, tumor-by-tumor pages
  • Physicians: tools for anonymised medical letters and treatment initiation
  • Assistants and secretaries: treatment duration and scheduling tools

Founder · Medical oncologist, attending physician (Chef de Clinique), CHUV Lausanne

Nicolas Desbaillets, MD, PhD

I am a medical oncologist at Lausanne University Hospital (CHUV), where I work as an attending physician with a supervisory role in the Department of Oncology. My clinical focus is neuro-oncology, thoracic oncology and melanoma.

I trained as an engineer before medicine. My PhD in cancer immunotherapy, on CAR-T cells and cancer stem cells, gave me a researcher's view of the questions clinicians face every day. Onconic grew out of that double perspective: good care depends on knowledge circulating between peers.

Behind Onconic

Nicolas Desbaillets

I work in medical oncology at Lausanne University Hospital (CHUV), mainly in neuro-oncology, thoracic oncology and cutaneous oncology. Before medicine, I trained as an engineer at EPFL and did a PhD in cancer immunotherapy.

Onconic started from a simple observation: every day we spend time looking for information colleagues have already found, or rebuilding tools that exist elsewhere. I wanted a place to share them. The site is not the work of one person: it is only worth what everyone brings to it.

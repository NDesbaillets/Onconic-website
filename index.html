Like a hive

Cancer care is a team effort.

In a hive, every bee has its role and information travels fast: through its dance, a forager shows the others where the flowers are. Oncology works the same way. Decisions rest on multidisciplinary discussion, treatments on shared protocols, and patient safety on fast and reliable communication between professionals.

01

Expertise

Medical oncology, radiation oncology, surgery, pathology, radiology, pharmacy, nursing, psycho-oncology and medical secretariats: each discipline brings essential expertise.

02

Shared decisions

From the tumour board to outpatient follow-up, important decisions are made together, based on guidelines (ESMO, NCCN) and the latest evidence.

03

Knowledge sharing

Key trials, protocols, good practice and template documents: spreading useful information reduces variability and saves time at the bedside. It is our way of showing where the flowers are.

04

Benefit for the patient

Safer, better coordinated and better explained treatments, with constant attention to toxicity and quality of life.

The patient journey

The patient at the centre, the team around.

From diagnosis to follow-up, every step involves several disciplines. The quality of care depends on how well they work together.

  1. DiagnosisImaging, biopsy, histology and molecular profiling establish the diagnosis and stage.
  2. DecisionThe tumour board sets the strategy according to guidelines, stage and the patient's profile.
  3. TreatmentSurgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, immunotherapy or targeted therapy, prescribed, prepared and monitored as a team.
  4. Follow-upResponse assessment, toxicity management, supportive care and rehabilitation.

Like a hive

Workers who collaborate, and dance.

A bee colony achieves together what no single bee could. Each has its role, all pursue the same goal, and information travels through dance: in a few moves, a forager shows the others where the flowers are. Cancer care works the same way.

01

The workers

Oncologists, nurses, pharmacists, medical assistants, radiation oncologists, surgeons, psycho-oncologists: each profession brings a skill the others lack.

02

Collaboration

Cancer treatment is a collective work, from the multidisciplinary board to follow-up at home. No important decision is made alone.

03

The dance

Sharing a good practice, a tool, a landmark trial or a well-written letter: that is how we show each other where the flowers are.

04

The honey

The purpose of all this work: patients who are better treated, better informed and better supported, in their treatment as in their quality of life.

Interprofessional by nature

The patient at the centre, the team around.

Fighting cancer and caring for patients' well-being are interprofessional by definition. They rely on clear communication and constant collaboration at every step of the journey.

  1. DiagnosisPathologists, radiologists and clinicians put the pieces together.
  2. DecisionThe tumour board combines expertise to propose the best treatment.
  3. TreatmentPhysicians, nurses, pharmacy and secretariats orchestrate every cycle.
  4. SupportSupportive care, rehabilitation and listening preserve quality of life.

In The Hive, members meet up at conferences and help each other. If you agree, your name is shown to other members on the events you attend, and they can reach you by email or WhatsApp. Without these options you remain “a discreet member”: you see the others, but they can neither recognise nor contact you, which makes collaboration much less fluid. You can change your mind at any time in My account.
